# STYLE — mandatory visual contract

Premium, photorealistic architectural product imagery; restrained half-gray monochrome. Never a desk setup, decorated workspace or lifestyle interior, except the approved-in-contract single premium walnut-support mode and opt-in pale-ivory repeated iPhone studio mode.

## HARD RULES — every photograph

- **Device count:** Exactly the devices requested by `layout_id` in `configs/presets.json`. **`environment-only` means ZERO devices**—show the empty architectural studio, materials and lighting only. No mock product, white-screen rectangle or object-like silhouette. `single`: exactly one iPhone 17 OR one MacBook Pro 14. `side-by-side` / `depth-staggered`: exactly TWO specified devices: **one MacBook Pro 14 plus one standard iPhone 17**, OR **two identical, open 14-inch MacBook Pros**. Two Macs are an intentional supported pair, not a generation error; no third Mac or iPhone. Never omit, substitute or add devices. **Only opt-in `repeated-grid` permits exactly SIX identical iPhone 17 **OR** exactly SIX identical iPhone 17 Pro units (never mixed models in one image) in a coherent diagonal 2×3 repetition on one surface**; it is a single-camera pattern, not a collage or six separate camera views. All other layouts retain exact 0/1/2-device rules. No 2×2 collage, split scene, contact sheet or multiple camera angles.
- **Geometry and screen:** Faithful device silhouette/proportions, MacBook hinge, keyboard/trackpad and screen; standard iPhone 17 (NOT Pro), bezel and centered Dynamic Island. **Every requested display (when present) completely visible and featureless pure white**, with no UI, text or prominent reflections. **Exception only for `repeated-grid`: central displays must be fully visible and blank pure white, while perimeter repeated phones may be deliberately cropped at image edges.** Do not obscure either screen or Mac keyboard in a pair.
- **Composition:** ONE standalone landscape **16:9** photograph with one coherent camera and scene. Respect `composition_id` plus `layout_id` for camera elevation/yaw, product size, relative placement, spacing, depth/occlusion and intentional negative space. No arbitrary crop or severe distortion. **In `repeated-grid` only**, edge cropping is intentional, but central phones are complete and the six device instances share one view, angle, scale and lighting. **The generic `high-view` composition and 90° bird's-eye flat lays are discontinued.** Exception: `mac-elevated-three-quarter` and `mac-chair-elevated` are Mac-only intentional oblique 30–40° studio/editorial views, never a 90° flat lay. Exception: when a SINGLE iPhone is lying face-up flat, position the camera at a controlled elevated 40–55° OBLIQUE angle to the supporting surface to reveal its whole screen, never directly overhead. Camera direction must follow the device pose. **For `repeated-grid` only**, oblique 40–50°, lower oblique 24–32°, and elevated oblique 58–68° are permitted; lower does not mean below the floor or losing the screen. The default single flat-iPhone 40–55° rule is unchanged.
- **Architectural surface examples:** For `environment-only` scenes, distinguish `vertical-wall`, `wall-floor-junction`, `wall-floor-corner`, `vertical-wall-and-floor`, and `horizontal-floor`. Preserve actual wall, floor or glass partitions as coherent architectural surfaces, not macro swatches, abstract texture tiles, or studio props. `horizontal-floor` examples show approx. 80–85% ground plane from a low forward-oblique camera and a minimal distant wall strip. Render **ZERO devices**.
- **Permitted device poses:** iPhone 17 alone may stand upright, lie fully flat face-up, lean against a credible integrated mineral support, or float independently with its white screen facing camera. A fully flat iPhone on a floor or block requires a diagonal elevated camera (40–55° above support) for complete screen visibility. MacBook Pro always remains OPEN, with raised screen; keyboard/trackpad visible except in the opt-in `mac-chair-front` true upward underside composition where camera geometry necessarily occludes part of the keyboard; the entire laptop must never be laid flat or closed. In mixed Mac+iPhone paired scenes, keep the Mac open and position the iPhone without obscuring either white screen. **In Mac+Mac paired scenes, BOTH laptops remain open, each showing its own fully visible pure-white display, black keyboard and trackpad.** Keep identical hardware body dimensions and believable perspective; left/right or shallow front/back spacing must not obscure either laptop.
- **Background:**- **Marble finishes:** Honed pale Carrara and graphite marble are allowed on coherent architectural wall, floor and intentional display-block surfaces. Require thin natural veins, plausible slab-scale continuity and restrained satin highlights. Avoid dramatic high-contrast veins or decorative interior furniture. Marble blocks are functional supports only in `on-block` or empty-stage reference scenes.
 Quiet grayscale industrial/mineral architecture, with 0–3 physically coherent architectural planes; minimal fiber-cement, microcement, plaster, slate, steel or integrated smoked glass. Spatial mode overrides generic floor cues.
- **Spatial modes:** `grounded`: product directly rests on a continuous mineral floor, with natural contact shadow. **Opt-in exception `furniture-supported`**: exactly ONE OPEN MacBook Pro rests securely on one substantial solid walnut TABLETOP or tall monolithic wooden BLOCK; complete physically stable contact, realistic wood scale and no chair or additional furniture. This is NOT `grounded` or `on-block` and cannot be used for iPhone, Mac + iPhone, two Macs or environment-only. `on-block`: one or two orthogonal matte-gray mineral plinths are allowed **only as functional product supports**, not decorative furnishings. If an iPhone is `flat-face-up`, the support must have one TABLE-SIZED broad horizontal top (about 80–140 cm across) extending around the phone, like a solid architectural concrete volume, never a tiny cube. If an iPhone is `tilted-supported`, an inconspicuous backrest may be an integrated facet of the same monolith. Ensure physical contact/stability and realistic shadows. `hover`: visible floor, slight elevation, delicate detached shadow. `suspended`: devices visibly unsupported in a **floorless image**. NO visible floor, horizon, wall-floor seam, podium, ground shadow, large cast shadow below devices or bottom-heavy platform. Use subtle vertical wall planes, tonal depth and light on the devices; never invent a floor just to establish scale.
- **Exclusions:** Only `repeated-grid` permits seamless pale-ivory backgrounds and intentional crop of identical iPhone edge instances; screens remain unbranded and blank. By default NO wood/woodgrain, desk, tabletop, furniture, hands/people, plants, coffee mugs, books, stationery, signage, stands, decorative supports, unrelated plinths/blocks (EXCEPT functional mineral display plinths when `on-block` is selected), logos, watermarks, unrelated objects or extra devices. **Only `furniture-supported` explicitly allows ONE substantial solid walnut TABLETOP or monolithic BLOCK as functional support, without chairs or other furniture.** Empty space stays empty.
- **Lighting:** Natural soft studio/daylight with restrained metallic and glass reflections, coherent gentle shadows; avoid theatrical directional beams unless specifically selected. In walnut-support scenes `window-cut-sun` permits coherent window-light shapes without screen glare. New `warm-grain-plaster` provides fine physical texture and sculpted daylight; `white-cyclorama` provides near-white infinity cove without texture, never a painted digital gradient.

## VARIATION and QA

Across candidates vary at least two material axes (layout/camera, architecture/material, lighting or spatial mode). Do not confuse a novel spatial arrangement with a collage.

**Hard reject**: any product/device in an `environment-only` scene, wrong device number/models, cropped/colored/overlaid screen outside the deliberate perimeter crop of `repeated-grid`, incorrect layout or occlusion, floor in `suspended`, missing shadow cue in `hover`, missing, implausible or undersized flat-iPhone support in `on-block`, closed/fully flattened Mac, generic high-view or 90° bird's-eye viewpoint, flat iPhone filmed at eye level so the screen disappears, camera/viewpoint not matching selected pose, wrong specified architecture/light, wood desk outside `furniture-supported`, chairs, plants or props inside it, or multiple independent views. Evaluate actual images separately from prompt integrity. CAD-perfect body geometry and pixel-perfect white are unverified without reference/measurement.
