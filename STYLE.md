# STYLE — mandatory visual contract

Premium, photorealistic architectural product imagery; restrained half-gray monochrome. Never a desk setup, decorated workspace or lifestyle interior.

## HARD RULES — every photograph

- **Device count:** Exactly the devices requested by `layout_id` in `configs/presets.json`. **`environment-only` means ZERO devices**—show the empty architectural studio, materials and lighting only. No mock product, white-screen rectangle or object-like silhouette. `single`: exactly one iPhone 17 OR one MacBook Pro 14. `side-by-side` / `depth-staggered`: exactly ONE of EACH; never duplicate, omit or substitute. No 2×2 collage, split scene, contact sheet or multiple camera angles.
- **Geometry and screen:** Faithful device silhouette/proportions, MacBook hinge, keyboard/trackpad and screen; standard iPhone 17 (NOT Pro), bezel and centered Dynamic Island. **Every requested display (when present) completely visible and featureless pure white**, with no UI, text or prominent reflections. Do not obscure either screen or Mac keyboard in a pair.
- **Composition:** ONE standalone landscape **16:9** photograph with one coherent camera and scene. Respect `composition_id` plus `layout_id` for camera elevation/yaw, product size, relative placement, spacing, depth/occlusion and intentional negative space. No arbitrary crop or severe distortion. **The generic `high-view` composition and 90° bird's-eye flat lays are discontinued.** Exception: when a SINGLE iPhone is lying face-up flat, position the camera at a controlled elevated 40–55° OBLIQUE angle to the supporting surface to reveal its whole screen, never directly overhead. Camera direction must follow the device pose.
- **Permitted device poses:** iPhone 17 alone may stand upright, lie fully flat face-up, lean against a credible integrated mineral support, or float independently with its white screen facing camera. A fully flat iPhone on a floor or block requires a diagonal elevated camera (40–55° above support) for complete screen visibility. MacBook Pro always remains OPEN, with raised screen and visible keyboard/trackpad; the entire laptop must never be laid flat or closed. In paired scenes, keep the Mac open and independently position the iPhone without obscuring either white screen.
- **Background:** Quiet grayscale industrial/mineral architecture, with 0–3 physically coherent architectural planes; minimal fiber-cement, microcement, plaster, slate, steel or integrated smoked glass. Spatial mode overrides generic floor cues.
- **Spatial modes:** `grounded`: product directly rests on a continuous mineral floor, with natural contact shadow. `on-block`: one or two orthogonal matte-gray mineral plinths are allowed **only as functional product supports**, not decorative furnishings. If an iPhone is `flat-face-up`, the support must have one TABLE-SIZED broad horizontal top (about 80–140 cm across) extending around the phone, like a solid architectural concrete volume, never a tiny cube. If an iPhone is `tilted-supported`, an inconspicuous backrest may be an integrated facet of the same monolith. Ensure physical contact/stability and realistic shadows. `hover`: visible floor, slight elevation, delicate detached shadow. `suspended`: devices visibly unsupported in a **floorless image**. NO visible floor, horizon, wall-floor seam, podium, ground shadow, large cast shadow below devices or bottom-heavy platform. Use subtle vertical wall planes, tonal depth and light on the devices; never invent a floor just to establish scale.
- **Exclusions:** NO wood/woodgrain, desk, tabletop, furniture, hands/people, plants, coffee mugs, books, stationery, signage, stands, decorative supports, unrelated plinths/blocks (EXCEPT functional mineral display plinths when `on-block` is selected), logos, watermarks, unrelated objects or extra devices. Empty space stays empty.
- **Lighting:** Natural soft studio/daylight with restrained metallic and glass reflections, coherent gentle shadows; avoid theatrical directional beams unless specifically selected.

## VARIATION and QA

Across candidates vary at least two material axes (layout/camera, architecture/material, lighting or spatial mode). Do not confuse a novel spatial arrangement with a collage.

**Hard reject**: any product/device in an `environment-only` scene, wrong device number/models, cropped/colored/overlaid screen, incorrect layout or occlusion, floor in `suspended`, missing shadow cue in `hover`, missing, implausible or undersized flat-iPhone support in `on-block`, closed/fully flattened Mac, generic high-view or 90° bird's-eye viewpoint, flat iPhone filmed at eye level so the screen disappears, camera/viewpoint not matching selected pose, wrong specified architecture/light, wood desk, unrelated props, or multiple independent views. Evaluate actual images separately from prompt integrity. CAD-perfect body geometry and pixel-perfect white are unverified without reference/measurement.

## VISUAL REFERENCE STATUS

Successful directions have copy-ready prompts in `prompts/` and optional metadata in `configs/presets.json > showcase_presets`. `user-approved-direction` means the art direction was liked, **not** proof of exact device geometry or perfect prompt handoff.
