# Lighting skill

- Choose a `lighting` entry from `configs/presets.json`; define the dominant source direction, hardness, angle, environment ambience and shadow behavior.
- Prefer broad diffused studio light, soft raking light and restrained natural daylight. Preserve subtle metallic edge highlights.
- Avoid hard/colored light, fake wet-looking floor reflections on matte mineral materials, heavy flares or exaggerated chiaroscuro.
- A white screen must remain readable despite natural glass reflections, and enough outline contrast must separate the device from a white wall.
- For each variation, consciously change lighting direction, mood or height; do not revert to the same diagonal beam in every output.
- Assess lighting and material plausibility on the **rendered image**, not just within the prompt.
