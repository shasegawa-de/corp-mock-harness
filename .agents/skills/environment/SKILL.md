# Environment skill

1. Read `STYLE.md` and `configs/presets.json`. Choose one dominant background material and up to three coherent architectural planes.
2. Explore fiber cement, diatomaceous plaster, board-formed concrete, microcement, slate, sandblasted concrete, brushed metal and smoked glass. Avoid overusing marble.
3. For `on-block`, permit one or two low, rectilinear concrete/fiber-cement blocks used ONLY to support the product(s); they are not unrelated decorative props. For `grounded` or `hover`, use continuous matte mineral floor and restrained architectural wall-floor transitions. For `suspended`, show **vertical architectural planes only**; no visible floor, horizon, ground shadow, stand or plinth. No freestanding blocks added to decorate or support a device.
4. Favor fine texture, low contrast and a soft gray tonal range. No natural curves, plants, scenery, furniture, colorful props or signage.
5. Differentiate variants by **spatial geometry** and depth as well as materials; changing marble to concrete while retaining identical light/camera is insufficient.
6. Reject any architectural element unrelated to the environment's coherent construction; a floating device needs no artificial support.
