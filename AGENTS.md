Read the COMPLETE individual files `AGENTS.md`, `STYLE.md`, `configs/devices.json`, `configs/presets.json` from `shasegawa-de/corp-mock-harness` GitHub `main`; parse and validate JSON. **Never use the versioned ZIP, excerpts, memory or guessed options.** If required content cannot be read, **STOP** and request it. A pinned commit is allowed only when explicitly requested.

## 2. PLAN — one photograph per Scene Contract

Default: one standard `iphone17`, four independent 16:9 images. Use `default_scenes` for unspecified four-image iPhone batches. Support `device_layouts` in `configs/presets.json`: `environment-only` = **ZERO devices and no screen/UI at all**; `single` = **exactly one specified device**; `repeated-grid` = **exactly six identical standard iPhone 17 devices**, a 2×3 diagonal array on one continuous matte surface with central screens fully visible and intentional outer-edge cropping only (not a collage); `side-by-side` and `depth-staggered` = **exactly the specified two devices**: either one MacBook Pro 14 plus one standard iPhone 17 (`pair`), or **two open, identical 14-inch MacBook Pros** (`mac-pair`). Never substitute an iPhone into a Mac+Mac scene or produce a third laptop. **One image, one camera/scene does NOT mean one device; environment-only can have zero devices.**

For each scene select valid `devices[]`, `pose_id` (single device or two identical Macs sharing a pose) or `poses` keyed by device (mixed pair) / physical left-right position (two identical Macs), `layout_id`, `spatial_mode_id`, `composition_id`, `environment_id`, `lighting_id` plus precise camera, positions, scale, 0–3 architectural planes and exclusions. `showcase_presets` are preferred example directions, not compulsory compositions; user instructions override a preset when coherent with hard constraints. Keep screen(s) entirely visible and featureless pure white. **Repeated-grid exception:** central iPhone screens stay entirely visible and blank; perimeter phones may be cut only by the image boundary; one coherent lens and identical orientation/size. For a two-device scene show both displays without obstruction, plus the keyboard and trackpad of **every** Mac. Two Macs use matched 14-inch physical proportions and remain independently open, with no overlap of screens/keyboards/trackpads.

**Environment-only mode:** When the request is for background/structure/material/lighting examples, select `layout_id=environment-only`, `devices=[]`, `composition_id=empty-stage`. Plan architecture and lighting ONLY: do not invent devices, white screens, mockups, props, text or product shadows. Valid spatial modes describe the empty stage itself: `grounded` (floor present), `on-block` (one intentional broad mineral plinth with no product), or `suspended` (floorless vertical planes; no ground shadow). Do not require or validate a `pose_id` for this case.

**Surface-only studies:** For architectural walls, floors, and their actual built junctions (not material swatches), use `surface_studies` in `configs/presets.json` with `devices=[]` and `layout_id=environment-only`. Preserve construction-scale plane geometry. `surface_type=horizontal-floor` requires `composition_id=floor-plane-emphasis`: floor occupies about 80–85% of the frame, camera low and oblique, only a narrow far wall band. For vertical walls and wall-floor junctions, keep panel seams and a physically credible planar intersection. Never generate abstract texture tiles or microscopic isolated materials when the user requested actual walls or flooring.

**Device pose is separate from placement.** For iPhone 17 in a `single` scene, `upright`, `flat-face-up`, `tilted-supported` or `suspended-angled` are valid `device_pose_rules.iphone17` values. **When the iPhone lies flat on the floor or on a block, raise/reposition the camera to a 40–55° oblique downward view relative to the horizontal support surface** so its ENTIRE pure-white screen is visible. The discontinued `high-view` preset remains forbidden as a generic design direction; this is a narrow pose-dependent exception, **not a vertical 90° bird's-eye shot**. For tilted phones, match the camera to the angled screen and show a plausible integrated point of support. **Repeated-grid ONLY:** six flat face-up matching phones use either 40–50° diagonal or 24–32° lower oblique camera while keeping central screens visible; no extra devices or split-view boards.

**MacBook Pro stays open.** Mac-only and paired scenes must show the raised white display, keyboard and trackpad. Its base may lie horizontally on a floor/block, but the **whole laptop must never be laid flat or closed**. A `flat-face-up` pose applies ONLY to iPhone; never mix it with a Mac pose.

Spatial modes: `grounded` = device rests directly on matte mineral floor; `on-block` = device rests on one or two rectilinear mineral presentation blocks (allowed functional supports, NOT decorative clutter). For a flat iPhone, the block must have a BROAD TABLE-SIZED mineral top, not a small cube; no table legs or furniture; `hover` = low above **visible** floor with faint separated shadow; `suspended` = **weightless, no floor, floor-wall seam, horizon, ground shadow or platform**. **Opt-in `furniture-supported`** = exactly ONE open MacBook Pro resting flat and securely on the seat of ONE natural walnut wooden chair; only this mode permits wood and furniture. Never apply to iPhone, paired devices or empty architecture. A `suspended` mode overrides floor references in an environment detail; prefer `suspended-mineral-planes`. Preserve quiet half-gray architectural depth. Never add desks, plants, mugs, hands, extra devices, logos or UI. No furniture/wood except the single functional chair under `furniture-supported`; no second chair, other décor or clutter.

**PRECHECK PASS** only after all four files, preset IDs, correct device count (six matching phones for `repeated-grid`), coherent spatial mode, no generic high-view camera (allow only the specified elevated oblique view for a flat iPhone), camera-angle/screen visibility matching pose, MacBook always open, and non-conflicting Scene Contract are verified. Verify `furniture-supported` is Mac single + one walnut chair and not contaminated by default mineral-floor rules. Identify selected IDs briefly before any generation.

## 3. RENDER-PROMPT LOCK

Batch request ≠ image-tool prompt. Freeze a **single-scene ~80–170-word English prompt** containing only that scene's devices, count, pose, relationship, pose-adapted camera, support geometry, architecture, spatial mode, lighting and exclusions. Start **"ONE standalone photorealistic product photograph. ONE camera view. Horizontal 16:9."** Then say **"NO products or devices"** for environment-only, **"Exactly one [specified model]"** for a single scene, or state the exact **two-device composition**: **one Mac + one iPhone**, or **two open 14-inch MacBook Pros with no iPhone**. In opt-in `repeated-grid`, state exactly **SIX identical flat face-up standard iPhone 17 devices**, with a regular diagonal 2×3 grid and intentional crop only at image edges; this is not a multi-view collage. Do not replace the specified models or number. Never send other scene options or a multiple-panel showcase. `prompts/` holds optional copy-ready fixtures.

If the effective tool prompt is inspectable, compare it with the frozen text. If it switches device count, placement, spatial mode, or asks for multiple views/collage, flag **`PROMPT_DRIFT` and STOP the batch**. If the tool hides/reformulates input, record **`PROMPT_UNVERIFIED`** instead of claiming an exact match. Where exact input fidelity is vital, use an explicit-prompt-capable interface.

**One locked prompt → one image-tool call → one standalone photograph.** Do not generate a contact sheet. Inspect the result before the next scene.

## 4. QA — prompt and image separately

Prompt fidelity: `MATCH / PROMPT_DRIFT / PROMPT_UNVERIFIED`. Inspect actual output: one camera frame, **exactly N requested devices and no others** (six for repeated-grid, central phones fully visible, perimeter crop allowed), correct device geometry and white screens **only when a device is requested**; for environment-only check absence of devices and correct architectural planes, materials, lighting and negative space; otherwise verify pose and pose-adapted viewpoint (oblique for a flat iPhone; open display for every Mac), physically credible support, specified layout/depth, correct spatial mode (direct floor contact for grounded, an actual mineral support for on-block, and NO floor for suspended), camera, material/architectural planes, light, no props except the explicitly allowed single walnut chair in `furniture-supported`, and ~16:9 (pixel verify if available). Hard failure → revise only that scene; prompt drift → repair input route. Mark unseen or unverifiable properties `UNVERIFIED`, never `PASS`.
